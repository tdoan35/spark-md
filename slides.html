Spark MD

02 · The challenge

A CT scan is hundreds of images, and hundreds more from prior scans.

  • Time consuming for radiologists to review each CT.
  • Careful attention needed to compare scans over time.
  • CT scans contain sensitive patient data.
Spark MD

03 · Spark MD

AI-assisted CT review, running locally.

  • Spark MD flags abnormalities in CT scans (e.g. lung nodules) for a radiologist to review.
  • It compares CT scans over time and estimates probability for cancer.
  • Inference runs locally on a Dell Pro Max with GB10.
Spark MD

04 · Why it matters

Help radiologists focus their review.

  • Brings a potential area of concern into view.
  • Compares that area across scans, and shows the change over time.
  • Local inference means the model call stays on the Dell Pro Max with GB10, instead of sending the scan to a cloud model.
